Privacy policy

Applies to WindX 1.0 and this website. Last updated 15 November 2026.

The short version: WindX does not collect, store or send any information about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry.

What stays on your PC

  • Your settings (theme, which controls are shown, your weather city) are saved in one small file at %AppData%\WindX\settings.json.
  • An error log may be written to %AppData%\WindX\error.log if something goes wrong. It is never uploaded anywhere.
  • What's playing, your volume, brightness, battery, CPU and memory are read from Windows to draw the island. They are shown on screen and then forgotten. Nothing is recorded.

Deleting the %AppData%\WindX folder removes everything WindX has ever saved.

The one thing that uses the internet

Weather is off by default. If you type a city in Settings, WindX asks Open-Meteo for that city's forecast:

  • once to look the city up by the name you typed, and
  • about every 30 minutes for the forecast, sending only that city's coordinates rounded to two decimal places.

No identifier, no account and nothing about your PC is included. As with any web request, Open-Meteo's servers can see your IP address; see their terms. Press Turn off in Settings and WindX makes no network requests at all.

What WindX can change on your PC

Only what you press: volume, mute, screen brightness, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on/off, media play/pause/skip, and opening the Windows snipping tool. If you switch on Start with Windows, it adds one entry under your own user's startup list, and removes it when you switch that off. WindX never asks for administrator rights.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics scripts and no third-party trackers. It is hosted on Netlify, which, like every web host, processes basic request data such as your IP address to deliver the pages; see Netlify's privacy policy.

Open source

WindX is released under the MIT licence. You don't have to take any of this on trust: the full source code is public and you can build the app yourself.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this page will say so before that version is released.

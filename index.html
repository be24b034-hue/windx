Lights out.

A detailed single-seater holds the racing line inside your island — wings, halo, spinning wheels, sparks off the floor. Pick from ten team colour schemes.

A timer and a full Pomodoro cycle live inside the island. While it runs, the pill counts down. Set reminders for any time of day and the island turns into a banner and chimes.

Download WindX 1.0 67 MB Lite version, 7 MB ›
Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit.

Download

Press Download WindX 1.0. Most people want this one — it needs nothing else.

Unzip

Right-click the zip in your Downloads folder and choose Extract All… → Extract.

Run

Double-click WindX.exe. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.

Make it yours

Click the island to open it. Right-click it for Settings, and turn on Start with Windows.

Why the SmartScreen warning? WindX isn’t code-signed yet (that costs money each year). It’s open source, and you can check your download against the SHA-256 below.
Lite version is just 7 MB but needs Microsoft’s free .NET 10 Desktop Runtime (x64) installed first.
Uninstall: right-click the island → Settings → Quit WindX, then delete the folder. To forget your settings too, delete %AppData%\WindX.
Tips: scroll on the island to change volume. Drag the hanging note and let go. Open the stopwatch tab for the timer and Pomodoro.
